Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Marketing In 3D ("we", "us") handles information on this website and in Marketing In 3D Studio (shown on Google sign-in as MarketingIn3D), our production tool that connects to YouTube.

Marketing In 3D Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who the app is for

Marketing In 3D Studio is an internal tool. Only the Marketing In 3D team uses it, and it signs in only to the Marketing In 3D YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When we sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): to read our channel details and set the titles, descriptions and tags of our videos.
  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to publish our finished Shorts.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views, watch time and audience retention for our videos.

How we use this data

  • We use it only to publish and improve the Marketing In 3D channel.
  • We do not sell it, rent it or use it for advertising.
  • We do not share it with anyone, except where the law requires it.
  • We do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token and any statistics the app downloads are stored on our own computer. They are not uploaded to any other server. The app saves its sign-in token as a file on our own computer. It does not place cookies or similar technology in anyone's browser.

How long we keep it

Downloaded statistics are kept no longer than needed. The app re-checks its authorisation at least every 30 days. Any other YouTube data it stores is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. If access is removed, we delete all stored YouTube data within 7 days.

How to remove access

Anyone who has signed in to the app can remove its access at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is removed, we can no longer read or change the channel, and we delete the stored token.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, forms or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The app and this website show no third-party content or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/B3nC123/marketingin3d/issues.
