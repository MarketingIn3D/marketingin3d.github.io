About our YouTube app

Marketing In 3D Studio

Marketing In 3D Studio (shown on Google sign-in as MarketingIn3D) is our own production tool. It connects to the Marketing In 3D YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services so we can publish and manage our videos. It is not offered to the public.

What it does
Uploads our finished videos, sets their titles, descriptions and tags, and reads our channel's view and audience statistics.
Whose data
Only the Marketing In 3D channel. The tool does not access other people's accounts.
Where data goes
Sign-in tokens and statistics stay on our own computer. We do not sell or share them.
